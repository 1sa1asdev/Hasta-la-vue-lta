### ENGLISH ONLY DZENKI

## Stand-up Meetings

- **Once a week** – full team together for sprint review & planning
- **Small team stand-ups** – within sub-groups as needed during the sprint
- **Working hours:** 9:00–17:00, weekdays – respect each other's time outside these hours
- If you can't make a meeting – notify the team in advance on Discord
- Every stand-up covers:
  1. What did I do?
  2. What will I do?
  3. Any blockers?

##  Communication Through Discord

- Discord is our main communication channel – keep it active and checked daily
- Use the right channels – don't mix code questions with memes (unless it's a great meme)
- Respond within a reasonable time during working hours – silence is not an answer
- If something is urgent, ping the person directly – don't just post and hope
- Be respectful and inclusive in all communication – written tone can be misread, assume good intent
- Big decisions are never made in DMs – bring it to the group channel so everyone is aligned

##  How to Manage Problems

- Understand the code you write – if you don't, ask before it becomes a bigger problem
- Stuck for long? Ask for help – no lone wolfing allowed
- Disagreements on decisions → team vote, majority wins, we commit and move on
- No blame culture – we fix problems, not people's personalities
- Be open to feedback – don't take it personally, it's about the code, not about you

## PR REQUIREMENTS
---
- At least 1 reviewer must approve the PR.
- Required status checks/tests must pass.
- PRs cannot be merged directly into main or development.
- Possibly the branch must be up to date before merging.
